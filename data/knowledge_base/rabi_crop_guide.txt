RABI CROP GUIDE — India Agricultural Advisory

RABI SEASON OVERVIEW
Rabi crops are sown in October–November after the kharif harvest and harvested in March–April. 
Major rabi crops: wheat, mustard, potato, chickpea (gram), lentil, barley, and peas.
Temperature during crop growth: 10–25°C. Requires mild winters and cool summers for proper grain filling.

KEY RABI CROPS AND BEST PRACTICES

1. WHEAT
- Sowing time: October 25–November 25 (North India). Timely sown = higher yield.
- Varieties: HD-3086, DBW-187 (rust resistant), PBW-550, GW-496 (Gujarat)
- Soil: Loamy to clay loam, pH 6.0–7.5
- Seed rate: 100–125 kg/ha. Seed treatment: Carboxin 75 WP @ 2.5 g/kg seed.
- Fertilizer: NPK 120:60:40 kg/ha. Apply full P and K at sowing; split N in 2–3 doses.
  First irrigation (Crown Root Initiation, 20–25 DAS) — apply 1/3 N.
  Second irrigation (Tillering, 40–45 DAS) — apply 1/3 N.
  Third irrigation (Jointing, 60–65 DAS) — apply remaining 1/3 N.
- Irrigation: 5–6 irrigations required. Critical stages: CRI, Tillering, Jointing, Flowering, 
  Milky grain, and Dough stage.
- Weed control: Isoproturon 75 WP @ 1 kg a.i./ha at 30–35 DAS for narrow-leaf weeds.
  Clodinafop-Propargyl 15 WP @ 60 g/ha for grassy weeds.
- Disease: Yellow Rust — spray Propiconazole 25 EC @ 1 ml/L at first appearance.
  Powdery Mildew — Hexaconazole 5 SC @ 2 ml/L.
- Harvest: When grain moisture reaches 12–14%; moisture meter recommended.

2. MUSTARD (Rapeseed-Mustard)
- Sowing time: October 1–20 (optimal). Late sowing reduces yield significantly.
- Varieties: RH-749, Varuna, Pusa Mustard 25, GSC-6 (Gujarat)
- Soil: Sandy loam, pH 6.0–8.0, well-drained
- Seed rate: 5–6 kg/ha, row spacing 30–45 cm
- Fertilizer: NPK 80:40:40 kg/ha + Sulphur 30 kg/ha (critical for oil quality).
  Apply full dose at sowing.
- Irrigation: 2–3 irrigations: at branching (30 DAS), flowering (45 DAS), pod filling (60 DAS).
- Aphid management: Spray Dimethoate 30 EC @ 1.5 ml/L when 25–26 aphids/plant.
  Avoid spraying during flowering to protect pollinators.
- White Rust: Spray Mancozeb 75 WP @ 2.5 g/L at first signs.
- Harvest: When 75% pods turn yellow-brown. Thresh immediately to prevent shattering.

3. POTATO
- Sowing time: October (plains); August–September (hills)
- Varieties: Kufri Sindhuri, Kufri Pukhraj, Kufri Badshah, Kufri Jyoti
- Soil: Sandy loam, pH 5.5–6.5 (slight acidity preferred)
- Seed rate: 2,000–2,500 kg/ha of certified mini-tubers or cut seed pieces.
  Treat with Indofil M-45 @ 3 g/L for 30 minutes before planting.
- Fertilizer: NPK 180:80:100 kg/ha + FYM 25 tonnes/ha.
  Apply full P, K, and 1/3 N at planting; apply remaining N at earthing-up (30 DAS).
- Irrigation: Maintain consistent soil moisture. Avoid drought stress at tuber initiation.
- Late Blight: Major disease. Spray Cymoxanil + Mancozeb @ 2.5 g/L preventively every 7–10 days
  during cool, humid weather.
- Harvest: When foliage yellows and desiccates (90–110 DAS). 
  Allow skin to set for 10–15 days before harvesting.

4. CHICKPEA (GRAM / CHANA)
- Sowing time: October 25–November 15 (North India)
- Varieties: JG-11, Pusa-372, JAKI-9218
- Soil: Medium black or red loam, pH 6.0–8.0. Avoid waterlogged soils.
- Seed rate: 80–100 kg/ha. Inoculate with Rhizobium + PSB culture.
- Fertilizer: NPK 20:60:20 kg/ha at sowing only. Rhizobium provides own nitrogen.
- Irrigation: 1–2 irrigations only: pod filling stage if rainfall absent.
- Wilt management: Use tolerant varieties. Drench with Carbendazim 1 g/L at first sign.
- Pod Borer: Spray Indoxacarb 15.8 EC @ 1 ml/L when 2–5% damage observed.

SOIL HEALTH MANAGEMENT (RABI)
- Test soil pH before sowing. Apply agricultural lime (calcitic) @ 2 t/ha for acidic soils.
- Green manuring with Dhaincha (Sesbania) before rabi season improves nitrogen by 60–80 kg/ha.
- Vermicompost @ 2–3 t/ha reduces chemical fertilizer requirement by 25–30%.
- Use Soil Health Card recommendations for fertilizer application.

POST-HARVEST MANAGEMENT
- Clean threshing floor to prevent contamination.
- Dry grain to safe moisture (12–14%) before storage.
- Store in metal bins with Aluminium Phosphide tablets (3 tablets per tonne) for pest control.
- Never store new grain on top of old grain.

MARKET LINKAGE
- Minimum Support Price (MSP): Set by CACP. Wheat MSP ₹2,275/qtl (2024-25).
  Mustard MSP ₹5,650/qtl; Potato not under MSP (market-determined).
- APMC mandis: Sell through regulated markets for fair price discovery.
- Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs): Join for collective bargaining and higher prices.
