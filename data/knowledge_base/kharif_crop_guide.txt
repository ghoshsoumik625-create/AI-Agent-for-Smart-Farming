KHARIF CROP GUIDE — India Agricultural Advisory

KHARIF SEASON OVERVIEW
Kharif crops are sown during the onset of the monsoon (June-July) and harvested in September-October. 
Major kharif crops include rice, maize, cotton, soybean, groundnut, and sugarcane.

KEY KHARIF CROPS AND BEST PRACTICES

1. RICE (Paddy)
- Sowing time: June to July (transplanting 25-30 days after nursery sowing)
- Soil: Clay loam, pH 5.5–7.0, well-waterlogged fields
- Seed rate: 40–50 kg/ha (direct seeding); 25–30 kg/ha (transplanting)
- Fertilizer: Apply 120:60:60 kg NPK/ha in split doses. Apply half nitrogen at transplanting, 
  quarter at tillering stage, quarter at panicle initiation.
- Water management: Maintain 2–5 cm standing water during vegetative stage.
- Pest management: Monitor for Brown Plant Hopper (BPH). Use resistant varieties (IR-64, MTU-7029).
  Spray Chlorpyriphos 20 EC @ 2.5 ml/L if BPH exceeds 5 per hill.
- Disease: Blast disease — spray Tricyclazole 75 WP @ 0.6 g/L at boot leaf stage.
- Harvest: When 80% of the grains turn golden yellow. Grain moisture 20–22%.

2. MAIZE
- Sowing time: June–July (after first monsoon rains)
- Soil: Well-drained loamy soil, pH 5.8–7.0
- Seed rate: 20–25 kg/ha, spacing 60x25 cm
- Fertilizer: NPK 150:75:75 kg/ha. Apply phosphorus and potassium at sowing; 
  split nitrogen in 3 doses.
- Irrigation: Critical at knee-high, tasseling, and grain filling stages.
- Fall Armyworm: India's major threat. Scout weekly. Apply Emamectin Benzoate 5 SG @ 0.4 g/L 
  at whorl stage infestation.
- Harvest: When husks turn brown and grain moisture drops to 25–30%.

3. COTTON
- Sowing time: May–June (rainfed); April–May (irrigated)
- Soil: Black cotton soil (vertisol), well-drained, pH 6.0–8.0
- Seed rate: 2.5 kg/ha (Bt cotton hybrid)
- Fertilizer: NPK 100:50:50 kg/ha + Zn 25 kg/ha + B 1 kg/ha. 
  Split nitrogen: 1/2 at sowing, 1/4 at squaring, 1/4 at boll development.
- Bollworm management: Use pink bollworm pheromone traps (5/ha). 
  Spray Indoxacarb 15.8 EC @ 1 ml/L at first boll infestation.
- Sucking pests (Whitefly, Jassids, Thrips): Spray Imidacloprid 17.8 SL @ 0.5 ml/L.
- Harvest: When 60–70% bolls open (3–4 pickings). Avoid picking during morning dew.

SOIL PREPARATION FOR KHARIF
- Deep plowing in April–May exposes soil to sun, kills pests, and improves structure.
- Apply Farm Yard Manure (FYM) 10–15 tonnes/ha and incorporate before last plowing.
- Soil test every 3 years; apply lime if pH < 5.5, gypsum if pH > 8.5.

IRRIGATION SCHEDULING
- Install tensiometer to monitor soil moisture (irrigate when tension > 40 cb for most crops).
- Drip irrigation reduces water use by 30–50% compared to flood irrigation.
- Avoid waterlogging — install field drains in heavy soils.

INTEGRATED PEST MANAGEMENT (IPM) PRINCIPLES
1. Use certified, disease-free seeds from government centers.
2. Practice crop rotation to break pest and disease cycles.
3. Plant border crops (sorghum, maize) to attract pest predators.
4. Use light traps (1/ha) to monitor and trap adult insects.
5. Introduce natural enemies — Trichogramma parasitoids for borers.
6. Chemical sprays only when pest population exceeds Economic Threshold Level (ETL).
7. Alternate pesticide groups to prevent resistance development.
